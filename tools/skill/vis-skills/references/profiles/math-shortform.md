# Mathematical shortform

Use this profile for a compact, self-contained mathematical motion study in which one parameter, recurrence, or reversible map drives the visible structure. The mathematics itself supplies the movement; the result is not a card sequence, compressed lecture, or generic high-energy edit.

## Define one relationship

State the single mathematical relationship, driving parameter and interval, independent source values, derived dependency graph, invariants, persistent subject, synchronized dependent views, repeated map or cycle, final visual conclusion, and whether the ending is a loop closure or a held one-shot result.

The short should combine four qualities:

1. a real geometric or quantitative subject changes;
2. graphs, vectors, labels, or alternate views derived from the same state remain synchronized;
3. repetition exposes a map, orbit, recurrence, phase relation, convergence, projection, or invariant;
4. the final pose communicates the relationship without explanatory paragraphs.

Not every short needs several views. Use only the views necessary to make the relationship inspectable, and let one dominate. Keep Text sparse: normally local identity labels, the essential formula or value, and one concise conclusion.

## Drive all evidence from one state

Move one parameter through representative states and derive every dependent mark at the same semantic moment. A vector, point, graph cursor, projection, area, tangent, trace, measurement, and numeric label that share a parameter must not drift into separately authored approximations.

Encode incidence and construction explicitly. Derive points on curves, intersections, tangent directions, transformed bases, accumulated traces, and result geometry from their actual operands. When a coordinate system changes, transform the owning Space or Group with the real matrix. Shared ownership keeps descendants together but does not prove the initial mathematical relationship; establish both the data dependency and transform ownership.

Before final choreography, perturb one independent value to an asymmetric valid case. Every claimed dependent must follow without editing another literal. Verify the invariant at representative intermediate states, not only at the beginning and end.

## Shape the kinetic arc

Establish the subject with little or no prose, move the driver slowly through the first distinct change, hold where the rule becomes readable, repeat or extend the transformation to reveal the pattern, and settle on the complete invariant or construction. This is a semantic arc, not a required number of shots.

Give each beat one dominant verb. Geometry leads; notation follows once the relationship is visible. Use equal-sampled Morph only when intermediate shapes express mathematical continuity; use target-state transforms for owners, bases, and rigid or affine families. Repetition must reveal a real relationship—decorative bounces, idle oscillation, or an unrelated camera move do not count.

Use linear motion when uniform parameter speed matters, ease-in-out for reversible transforms, and restrained easing for construction. Accelerate repetition only after the first case is understandable. Hold the resolved relationship long enough to inspect at the exact final time.

Default to `loop=false` when the construction has a natural conclusion. Use `loop=true` only for a requested or mathematically meaningful recurrence whose object state, camera, labels, accumulated trace, and salience return exactly to the intended initial boundary. An A-to-B-to-A motion is not automatically explanatory; the return must preserve or expose the claimed structure.

## Acceptance

- The short is understandable in one viewing without reading a paragraph.
- The mathematical subject occupies the visual emphasis rather than presentation chrome.
- At least one change or repetition reveals the stated relationship.
- All synchronized views agree at every encoded frame.
- The asymmetric dependency perturbation updates every claimed dependent and no unrelated presentation constant.
- Motion settles often enough to perceive structure and never ends by erasing the result.
- The final still communicates the conclusion without playback.
